Contact page was added in Contentful via the GoogleForm content-type hack. It renders
at /en/contact. During this session we diagnosed and fixed why its embedded Google Form
was rendering as a raw unstyled iframe instead of the site-styled form: the form's
`formId` in Contentful was the public share-link ID (`1FAIpQLS...`) instead of the
Forms-API edit ID, and the form wasn't shared with the service account. Both were
corrected by the user; the styled form now renders. Given the last comment on this issue
predates that fix, this is probably safe to close or verify-and-close rather than pick up
as new work - confirm with @wynnmade before closing since they may want to double check
deployment.
